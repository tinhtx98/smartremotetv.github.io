1. About the App

Smart Mirror Screen & Cast TV lets you mirror your phone screen to a smart TV or compatible display, and cast photos, videos, and audio from your device to that display over your local Wi-Fi network. Mirroring and casting happen directly between your device and your TV on your local network. We do not receive, store, or have access to the content you mirror or cast.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or address to use the App. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, screen size, and system settings relevant to compatibility.
  • Usage information: which features you use, screens viewed, button interactions, session length, and app version.
  • Diagnostic information: crash reports, error logs, and performance data used to find and fix bugs.
  • Identifiers: advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID), app instance ID, and similar identifiers that do not directly identify you.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (country or region level only). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Purchase information: status of your subscription or purchase, processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

2.3 Information related to your local network

To find and connect to TVs and display devices, the App scans your local Wi-Fi network. It may process device names, model information, and local network addresses (such as IP addresses) of nearby compatible devices. This information is used only to establish the connection, and is kept on your device. We do not collect the names or contents of other devices on your network.

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store the video, audio, or images shown during screen mirroring.
  • We do not collect the photos, videos, or music you cast.
  • We do not collect your contacts, call logs, or SMS messages.
  • We do not collect precise location, biometric, or health data.

3. Screen Mirroring and Casting Data

  • Screen mirroring: when you start mirroring, Android asks for your permission to capture your screen. Everything displayed on your screen, including notifications and private information, may be visible on the TV while mirroring is active. Stop mirroring before opening sensitive content.
  • Direct transfer: mirrored and cast content is sent directly from your device to your TV over your local network. It does not pass through our servers.
  • No recording: we do not record, save, or analyze your mirrored screen or cast media.
  • Audio: where the App transmits device audio together with the screen, it is used only for playback on the TV and is not stored.
  • Third-party receivers: the TV or display you connect to is operated by its manufacturer, and its handling of the content is governed by that manufacturer's own privacy policy.

4. App Permissions

The App may request the following permissions. Each is used only for the purpose described.

PermissionPurpose
Internet / network state To display ads, process purchases, send analytics and crash reports, and check connectivity.
Wi-Fi state and multicast / nearby devices To discover TVs and display devices on your local network and connect to them.
Screen capture (system prompt) To mirror your screen to the TV. Android asks for your consent each time mirroring starts.
Photos, videos, and audio (media access) To let you browse and select media on your device to cast to the TV. Files are not uploaded to us.
Microphone (where applicable) To transmit audio during mirroring when you choose to enable it. Audio is not recorded or stored.
Foreground service and notifications To keep mirroring or casting running in the background and show a notification while it is active.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device's system settings. Some features may not work without them.

5. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain screen mirroring and casting features.
  • To analyze and improve performance, stability, and user experience.
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes, bugs, and connection issues.
  • To show advertisements and measure their effectiveness.
  • To process and verify in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • To respond to your support requests and inquiries.
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these situations:

  • Service providers: third parties that help us run the App (analytics, crash reporting, advertising, billing), who may process data only on our behalf or under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • Protection of rights: to enforce our terms, protect our rights, users, or the public, or investigate fraud or security issues.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you of any change in how your information is handled.
  • With your consent: in any other case where you have given us permission.

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their policies.

8. Advertising

The App may display ads provided by Google AdMob and its partners. These services may use your advertising ID and device information to serve and measure ads, and may show personalized ads based on your interests where permitted by law.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we request your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Opting out does not remove ads; the ads you see will be less relevant.

9. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium features may be offered through in-app purchases or subscriptions processed by Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your full payment details. We receive only the information needed to confirm that your purchase is active. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

10. Data Retention

We keep analytics and diagnostic data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. Information stored locally on your device, such as recently connected TVs, is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to resolve your request.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) where applicable. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Because mirroring and casting take place over your local network, we recommend using a trusted, password-protected Wi-Fi network and avoiding public networks when sharing private content.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
